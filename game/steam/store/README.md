# Steam store assets — 뿌리째 털어라 / Uproot Heist

Generated by `tools/steam-assets/` (see its README). Screenshots and plates are real in-engine
frames of the shipping build; the wordmark is the real title-screen logo. Capsules carry the logo
only: no slogans, review quotes, awards or prices. The library hero and the page background carry
no logo or text.

**Capsule key art** (all five capsules, the library hero and the page background): the game's
real title diorama — the same bank, roots, rope, raccoon crew, van and FX the title screen plays —
caught at the heave just before the bank pops, re-lit for night with the game's own sky dome
(indigo toy clouds, stars, sunburst) per `docs/STEAM_RELEASE.md` ("밤하늘 남보라 + 금색 은행"), and
framed by a camera of its own per capsule shape. It is key art, not a gameplay frame: the title
screen itself plays at dusk, and the bank is shown walls-up (menus cut the camera-side walls
away). On the night capsules the wordmark gets a cream die-cut sticker edge so its ink outlines
do not sink into the sky.

- `logo/logo_{ko,en}.png` — trimmed transparent wordmarks at full resolution (KO: 뿌리째 털어라 with
  the UPROOT HEIST ribbon, exactly as on the title screen; EN: the same lockup with UPROOT HEIST
  in the big letters and the Korean title on the ribbon).
- `plates/{ko,en}/` — clean plates (no HUD) of the staged moments: whole-bank uproot (strain, pop,
  snap, after), steal from a moving bank, fence bust, police chase / tackle, final-30-s chase,
  bank recovery, title diorama (16:9, tall, hero), layout preview, rival stage and ladder.
- `screenshots/{ko,en}/extra/` — more real frames (title screen, tournament ladder, bank
  recovery, the final-30-s siren banner) for rotation.
- App icons for the builds live in `build/icon.png`, `build/icon.ico`, `build/icons/*`.

Screenshot order (both languages): 01 match start · 02 whole-bank uproot · 03 steal out of a
moving bank · 04 police chase · 05 fence bust · 06 final 30 s (getaway police wave) ·
07 results with the biggest event · 08 rival tournament (counter line before the next game) ·
09 3D layout preview.

Staged moments (02–06) happen inside real matches after 14–40 s of real play by the game's own
AI, so the clock, the scores and the police timers read like a match in progress. 07 and 08 are
whole matches played out by the game's AI with nothing staged.
